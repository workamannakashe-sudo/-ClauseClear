LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is made and entered into at Mumbai on this 1st day of October, 2025, by and between:

Mr. Rajesh Sharma, residing at Flat 402, Sea Breeze Heights, Bandra West, Mumbai - 400050, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR" (which expression shall unless repugnant to the context include his heirs, executors, and assigns) of the ONE PART;

AND

Ms. Riya Patel, residing at B-12, Green Acres, Andheri East, Mumbai - 400069, hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE" (which expression shall unless repugnant to the context include her legal heirs) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Licensor is the lawful owner of the residential premises situated at Flat No. 301, 3rd Floor, Palm Grove Apartments, Khar West, Mumbai - 400052 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensed Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IT IS MUTUALLY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. TERM AND TENURE:
The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee the leave and license to occupy and use the Licensed Premises for a fixed period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st October 2025 and expiring on 31st August 2026.

2. MONTHLY LICENSE FEE:
The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of ₹35,000 (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand only) payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each English calendar month via electronic bank transfer.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Licensee has deposited with the Licensor an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹70,000 (Rupees Seventy Thousand only), equivalent to two (2) months' license fee. The Licensor shall refund the entire deposit amount within seven (7) banking days of peaceful handover of vacant possession at the expiry of this agreement, subject only to deductions for unpaid utility charges or verified physical damages exceeding normal wear and tear.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND TERMINATION NOTICE:
Both parties agree to an initial lock-in period of three (3) months. After completion of the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice or payment of one month's license fee in lieu thereof.

5. SOCIETY CHARGES AND UTILITY BILLS:
The Licensor shall be responsible for payment of all regular society maintenance charges, property taxes, and municipal cess. The Licensee shall pay for electricity, piped gas, and high-speed internet consumed during the subsistence of this license based on actual meter bills.

6. INSPECTION AND NOTICE OF ENTRY:
The Licensor or authorized representatives shall have the right to inspect the Licensed Premises during reasonable daytime hours (between 10:00 AM and 7:00 PM) only after providing at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written or electronic notice to the Licensee, except in case of genuine unforeseen emergencies.

7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE:
Day-to-day minor repairs up to ₹1,000 (such as replacement of electrical fuses, light bulbs, and tap washers) shall be borne by the Licensee. Major structural repairs, seepage, plumbing line failures, and pre-existing electrical wiring issues shall be repaired and borne solely by the Licensor within forty-eight (48) hours of written notification.

8. REGISTRATION AND STAMP DUTY:
The cost of stamp duty and government e-registration fees under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 shall be shared equally (50:50) by the Licensor and the Licensee.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Licensor and the Licensee have signed this agreement on the day and year first written above.
