LEASE RENEWAL & AMENDMENT AGREEMENT (VERSION B)

1. AMENDMENT & PARTIES
This Lease Renewal and Modification Agreement amends the prior Residential Lease dated June 1, 2025 between Oakwood Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant") for Apt 3B, 742 Evergreen Terrace.

2. RENEWAL TERM
The lease term is extended for an additional twelve (12) months, commencing July 1, 2026 and expiring June 30, 2027.

3. RENT INCREASE & NEW FEES
Monthly rent is increased from $1,800.00 to $2,050.00 (a 13.9% increase). Additionally, Tenant shall pay a new mandatory monthly "Community Amenities & Trash Fee" of $85.00, not previously assessed.

4. REDUCED GRACE PERIOD & INCREASED LATE CHARGE
The grace period is reduced from five (5) calendar days to three (3) calendar days. The late fee is increased from $50.00 to $125.00.

5. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL ESCALATION
Tenant must provide ninety (90) days' advance written notice of non-renewal prior to lease end (increased from the original 60-day requirement). Failure to provide 90 days' notice will automatically convert the lease to month-to-month at an escalated rate equal to 150% of the base rent ($3,075.00/mo).

6. AIR CONDITIONING MAINTENANCE OBLIGATION
Tenant is now required to arrange and pay for semi-annual HVAC filter replacements and servicing by a licensed HVAC technician, providing receipts to Landlord twice annually.

7. PET POLICY MODIFICATION
The monthly pet fee is increased from $25.00 to $50.00 per month.
