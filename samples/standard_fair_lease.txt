RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (STANDARD / BALANCED)

1. PARTIES & PREMISES
This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on June 1, 2025, between Oakwood Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant") for the premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apt 3B, Springfield.

2. LEASE TERM
The initial term shall commence on July 1, 2025 and expire on June 30, 2026. Either party may terminate or propose renewal terms upon providing at least sixty (60) days' written notice prior to the expiration date.

3. RENT & LATE CHARGES
Tenant agrees to pay rent of $1,800.00 per month, payable in advance on the 1st calendar day of each month. A grace period of five (5) calendar days is permitted. If rent is received after 11:59 PM on the 5th day, a late fee of $50.00 shall be assessed.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $1,800.00 (equivalent to one month's rent) held in an interest-bearing escrow account. The deposit will be returned within thirty (30) days following move-out, minus itemized deductions for damages exceeding ordinary wear and tear.

5. LANDLORD ENTRY & NOTICE
Landlord and authorized agents shall have the right to enter the premises for routine inspections, maintenance, or showings upon providing at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance written or electronic notice. In cases of bona fide emergencies endangering life or property, entry without prior notice is permitted.

6. MAINTENANCE & REPAIRS
Landlord shall be responsible for maintaining structural elements, exterior walls, roof, electrical systems, plumbing, and heating systems in good operating condition in accordance with applicable local housing codes. Tenant shall keep the interior clean, promptly report leaks or defects, and be responsible for damage caused by willful neglect.

7. SUBLETTING & ASSIGNMENT
Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the premises without obtaining Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

8. PET POLICY
One domestic cat or dog under 35 lbs is permitted upon payment of a refundable pet deposit of $250.00 and an additional monthly pet fee of $25.00.

9. QUIET ENJOYMENT & RULES
Tenant shall be entitled to the peaceful and quiet enjoyment of the premises. Reasonable noise restrictions apply between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
